    3-Step Setup & WiFi Upload

    Zero third-party libraries. Only the official Espressif ESP32 board package is needed in Arduino IDE.

    1. Flash the WiFi Player once: Download ESP32_OLED_WiFi_Player.ino from the WiFi Player tab, select Partition Scheme No OTA (2MB APP / 2MB SPIFFS), wire the OLED (), and upload.
    2. Convert media to .olv: Drop a video, GIF, or image (or tap a sample clip) on the Convert tab and download the compressed .olv file. Optionally pick a WS2812B LED effect, colour and LED count on the same tab.
    3. Upload over WiFi: Join WiFi ESP32-OLED (password 12345678) or scan the QR codes, visit http://192.168.4.1, and upload. The LEDs follow the effect saved in that file, or the colour you pick live in the “WS2812B LEDs — Live Colour” card on that page. Adjust OLED brightness live on the page or tap the ESP32 onboard BOOT button to switch videos.

    Wiring 60+ WS2812B LEDs (separate power supply)
    1. Use a dedicated 5 V supply for the strip. Budget about 60 mA per LED at full white (60 LEDs ≈ 3.6 A, 150 LEDs ≈ 9 A); the LED Brightness slider lowers this a lot.
    2. Supply +5 V → strip VDD, supply GND → strip GND, and also join the supply GND to an ESP32 GND pin (common ground). Do not connect the supply +5 V to the ESP32.
    3. ESP32 LED data GPIO → strip DIN through a ~330 Ω resistor, kept short. Add a 1000 µF capacitor across the strip’s +5 V/GND where the supply connects.
    4. The ESP32 outputs 3.3 V logic. Many strips accept it, but if the first LEDs flicker or show random colours, add a 74AHCT125 / 74HCT245 level shifter on the data line.
    5. Connect GND first and switch on the LED supply together with (or before) the ESP32.
    OLED stays blank or upload fails?
    1. Swap-check SDA and SCL wires against the pin readout under the preview.
    2. Try switching Screen Chip between SSD1306 and SH1106 (many 1.3" screens use SH1106).
    3. Check I2C address: most modules use 0x3C, some use 0x3D.
    4. If uploads fail, confirm Partition Scheme is No OTA (2MB APP / 2MB SPIFFS) and delete older videos on 192.168.4.1.
